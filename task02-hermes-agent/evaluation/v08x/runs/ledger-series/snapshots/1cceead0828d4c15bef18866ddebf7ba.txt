<p id="mwCA"><b id="mwCQ">2030 Bilingual Nation</b> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese" title="Traditional Chinese" class="mw-redirect" id="mwCg">Traditional</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters" title="Traditional Chinese characters" about="#mwt2" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwCw" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"zh","href":"./Template:Zh"},"params":{"t":{"wt":"2030雙語國家"},"p":{"wt":"2030 Shuāngyǔ Guójiā"}},"i":0}}]}'>Chinese</a><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt2">:</span><span about="#mwt2"> </span><span lang="zh-Hant" about="#mwt2"><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_traditional_Chinese-language_text"/>2030雙語國家</span><span about="#mwt2">; </span><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin" title="Pinyin" about="#mwt2">pinyin</a><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt2">:</span><span about="#mwt2"> </span><i about="#mwt2" id="mwDA"><span lang="zh-Latn">2030 Shuāngyǔ Guójiā</span></i>; <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade–Giles" title="Wade–Giles" id="mwDQ">Wade–Giles</a>: <i id="mwDg">2030</i> <i id="mwDw">Shuang<sup id="mwEA">1</sup>-yü<sup id="mwEQ">3</sup></i> <i id="mwEg">Kuo<sup id="mwEw">2</sup>-chia<sup id="mwFA">1</sup></i>; <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pe̍h-ōe-jī" title="Pe̍h-ōe-jī" about="#mwt3" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwFQ" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"zh","href":"./Template:Zh"},"params":{"poj":{"wt":"2030 Siang-gí Kok-ka"}},"i":0}}]}'>Pe̍h-ōe-jī</a><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt3">:</span><span about="#mwt3"> </span><i about="#mwt3" id="mwFg"><span lang="nan-Latn">2030 Siang-gí Kok-ka</span></i>; <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pha̍k-fa-sṳ" title="Pha̍k-fa-sṳ" id="mwFw">Pha̍k-fa-sṳ</a>: <i id="mwGA">2030 Sûng-ngî Koet-kâ</i>) is a policy in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan" title="Taiwan" id="mwGQ">Taiwan</a> promulgated by the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ing-wen" title="Tsai Ing-wen" id="mwGg">Tsai Ing-wen</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_Ching-te" title="Lai Ching-te" id="mwGw">Lai Ching-te</a> presidential administrations. Its goal is to make <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mwHA">English</a> and another language (no official confirmation yet) the primary languages in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Taiwan" title="Culture of Taiwan" id="mwHQ">Taiwanese society</a> by 2030. In 2014, the then-mayor of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tainan" title="Tainan" id="mwHg">Tainan</a> city Lai Ching-te launched a ten-year plan entitled "English as the second <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language" title="Official language" id="mwHw">official language</a>" (英語為第二官方語言) to transform Tainan into a bilingual city by 2024. Lai Ching-te further promoted the national <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingual" title="Bilingual" class="mw-redirect" id="mwIA">bilingual</a> policy after taking over as <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_the_Republic_of_China" title="Premier of the Republic of China" class="mw-redirect" id="mwIQ">premier of the Executive Yuan</a> in 2017, as <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_Republic_of_China" title="Vice President of the Republic of China" id="mwIg">vice president</a> of Tsai Ing-wen in 2020, and finally as president in 2024. Tsai Ing-wen repeatedly mentioned the goal of being a bilingual country by 2030 in her presidential inaugural address. As of 2021, a budget of more than <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Taiwan_dollar" title="New Taiwan dollar" id="mwIw">NT$</a>10 billion (over four years) has been allocated to implement this policy.<sup about="#mwt6" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-cw-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwJA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwJQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJg">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwNg">The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan" title="Taiwan" id="mwNw">Republic of China (Taiwan)</a> does not have an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language" title="Official language" id="mwOA">official language</a> based on its laws. The <a rel="mw:WikiLink/Interwiki" href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/國家語言發展法" title="zh:國家語言發展法" class="extiw" id="mwOQ">National Languages Development Act</a> recognizes twenty "<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_language" title="National language" id="mwOg">national languages</a>" (國家語言), but <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Chinese" title="Standard Chinese" id="mwOw">Standard Chinese</a> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandarin_Chinese" title="Mandarin Chinese" id="mwPA">Mandarin</a>) is the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_facto" title="De facto" class="mw-redirect" id="mwPQ">de facto</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca" title="Lingua franca" id="mwPg">lingua franca</a>. Standard Mandarin was mainly brought to <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formosa" title="Formosa" class="mw-redirect" id="mwPw">Taiwan</a> by the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuomintang" title="Kuomintang" id="mwQA">Kuomintang</a> when its seat of government moved to the island. It implemented a Mandarin language policy which banned the use of spoken <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Hokkien" title="Taiwanese Hokkien" id="mwQQ">Taiwanese Hokkien</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Hakka" title="Taiwanese Hakka" id="mwQg">Hakka</a>, and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Taiwan" title="Languages of Taiwan" id="mwQw">other languages</a> in schools or in official settings used by most <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_people" title="Taiwanese people" id="mwRA">Taiwanese</a> at that time; violations of the prohibition in schools often resulted in physical punishments, fines, or humiliation.<sup about="#mwt17" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-2" id="mwRQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwRg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRw">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwSA">]</span></span></a></sup> Until the present times, Standard Mandarin speakers tend to be politically aligned to the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-Blue_Coalition" title="Pan-Blue Coalition" id="mwSQ">Pan-Blue Coalition</a>, while Taiwanese Hokkien speakers tend to support the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-Green_Coalition" title="Pan-Green Coalition" id="mwSg">Pan-Green Coalition</a>.<sup about="#mwt20" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-3"}}'><a href="#cite_note-3" id="mwSw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwTA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwTQ">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwTg">]</span></span></a></sup> Some users of Taiwanese Hokkien believe that their language is still being suppressed by a language coming from the outside, Standard Mandarin.<sup about="#mwt23" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-4" id="mwTw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwUA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwUQ">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwUg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwUw"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mwVA">English</a> doesn't have a long history of wide usage in Taiwan but it is the most commonly used language internationally after <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II" title="World War II" id="mwVQ">WWII</a>. Around the globe, 56 countries use <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_territories_where_English_is_an_official_language" title="List of countries and territories where English is an official language" id="mwVg">English as an official language</a>. Almost all have had a long history of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire" title="British Empire" id="mwVw">British</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_imperialism" title="American imperialism" id="mwWA">American colonization</a>, except <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwanda" title="Rwanda" id="mwWQ">Rwanda</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burundi" title="Burundi" id="mwWg">Burundi</a>. If Taiwan succeeds with its <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingualism" title="Bilingualism" class="mw-redirect" id="mwWw">bilingual</a> policy, it will become the first country in the world to become bilingual from a top-down approach.<sup about="#mwt26" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ltnJiangHe_5-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ltnJiangHe"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-ltnJiangHe-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ltnJiangHe-5" id="mwXA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwXQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXg">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwYA">According to a survey, 87% of the Taiwanese people support English as the second official language, and 89% support the opening of bilingual English classes in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_schooling" title="Public schooling" class="mw-redirect" id="mwYQ">public</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_school" title="Primary school" id="mwYg">primary</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_school" title="Secondary school" id="mwYw">secondary schools</a>.<sup about="#mwt29" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwZA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwZQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZg">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZw">]</span></span></a></sup> Moreover, this policy has cross-party support in both <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party" title="Political party" id="mwaA">political parties</a>. An overwhelming majority of the public supports English as the second official language, regardless if they are pro-blue or pro-green, support <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_unification" title="Chinese unification" id="mwaQ">reunification</a> or the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Chinas" title="Two Chinas" id="mwag">status quo</a>, or pro-<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_independence_movement" title="Taiwan independence movement" id="mwaw">independence</a>.<sup about="#mwt32" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-NewsLens-7"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mwbA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwbQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwbg">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwbw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwcA">Some cities in Taiwan have attempted to implement English as a common, official language in the past. In 2014, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_Ching-te" title="Lai Ching-te" id="mwcQ">Lai Ching-te</a>, then <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Tainan" title="Mayor of Tainan" id="mwcg">Mayor of Tainan</a>, launched a ten-year plan to make English the second official language.<sup about="#mwt33" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwcw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwdA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwdQ">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwdg">]</span></span></a></sup> In 2019, the then-<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Kaohsiung" title="Mayor of Kaohsiung" id="mwdw">Mayor of Kaohsiung City</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Kuo-yu" title="Han Kuo-yu" id="mweA">Han Kuo-yu</a> also promoted <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingualism" title="Bilingualism" class="mw-redirect" id="mweQ">bilingualism</a> in the city. He had all road signs and signages changed to bilingual signs and required that after two years, all civil servants, teachers, police officers, and firefighters must pass an English test for promotion.<sup about="#mwt36" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-money_8-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"money"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-money-8"}}'><a href="#cite_note-money-8" id="mweg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwew"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwfA">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwfQ">]</span></span></a></sup> In the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Taiwanese_presidential_election" title="2020 Taiwanese presidential election" id="mwfg">2020 presidential election</a>, Han Kuo-yu listed <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingual_education" title="Bilingual education" id="mwfw">bilingual education</a> as one of his <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_agenda" title="Political agenda" id="mwgA">political agenda</a>.<sup about="#mwt37" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwgQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwgg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwgw">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwhA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="2" id="mwhQ" aria-labelledby="Rationale"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Rationale">Rationale</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Rationale"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>

<section data-mw-section-id="3" id="mwhg" aria-labelledby="Economy"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Economy">Economy</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Economy"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwhw">In the policy's development blueprint issued by the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Yuan" title="Executive Yuan" id="mwiA">Executive Yuan</a>, the two major goals of the policy are to "cultivate the English proficiency of the people" (厚植國人英語力) and "enhance national competitiveness" (提升國家競爭力).<sup about="#mwt40" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-9" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-9"}}'><a href="#cite_note-9" id="mwiQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwig"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwiw">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwjA">]</span></span></a></sup> One of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vision_statement" title="Vision statement" id="mwjQ">visions</a> presented is to "provide people with high-quality jobs and enhance Taiwan's economic development" (提供人民優質工作機會，提升臺灣經濟發展).<sup about="#mwt43" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-lazypack_10-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"lazypack"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-lazypack-10"}}'><a href="#cite_note-lazypack-10" id="mwjg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwjw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkA">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwkg">An editor of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Times" title="Liberty Times" id="mwkw">Liberty Times</a> believes that to strengthen Taiwan's competitiveness in the international market, English proficiency is necessary. This can attract <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_direct_investment" title="Foreign direct investment" id="mwlA">foreign investments</a> and allow international banks to set up financial centers, especially foreign businessmen who are leaving <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong" title="Hong Kong" id="mwlQ">Hong Kong</a> because of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_China" title="Government of China" class="mw-redirect" id="mwlg">Mainland Chinese government</a>.<sup about="#mwt46" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-LtnEditorial_11-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"LtnEditorial"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-LtnEditorial-11"}}'><a href="#cite_note-LtnEditorial-11" id="mwlw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwmA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwmQ">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwmg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwmw">According to a survey by Chun Shin Ltd., the organization conducting <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TOEIC" title="TOEIC" id="mwnA">TOEIC</a> tests in Taiwan, nearly 60% of enterprises have put more focus into English in the past three years than before. If the TOEIC test is used to measure English proficiency, the average score of TOEIC test-takers in Taiwan in 2020 is 564, which is lagging behind Hong Kong (589), <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia" title="Malaysia" id="mwnQ">Malaysia</a> (634), and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea" title="South Korea" id="mwng">South Korea</a> (683). Andrew Wylegala, President of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Chamber_of_Commerce_in_Taiwan" title="American Chamber of Commerce in Taiwan" id="mwnw">American Chamber of Commerce in Taiwan</a>, thinks that if Taiwan is to compete with regional financial centers such as <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore" title="Singapore" id="mwoA">Singapore</a> and Hong Kong, a bilingual policy is essential. A study by the English department of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_Normal_University" title="National Taiwan Normal University" id="mwoQ">National Taiwan Normal University</a> found that the number of words taught in English classes in Taiwan is lower than in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China" title="China" id="mwog">mainland China</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea" title="South Korea" id="mwow">South Korea</a>, and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan" title="Japan" id="mwpA">Japan</a>.<sup about="#mwt49" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-12" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-12"}}'><a href="#cite_note-12" id="mwpQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwpg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwpw">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwqA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="4" id="mwqQ" aria-labelledby="National_Defense"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="National_Defense">National Defense</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: National Defense"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwqg">The bilingual policy safeguards Taiwan's sovereignty by promoting exchanges between the Taiwan (ROC) government and its people with other <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English-speaking_world" title="English-speaking world" id="mwqw">English-speaking countries</a> and thereby deepening its relationship with them at a time they are all experiencing tensions in their relationships with China.<sup about="#mwt50" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mwrA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwrQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwrg">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwrw">]</span></span></a></sup> At a seminar entitled  "Taiwan-US Education Initiative: Establishing a Global Education Partnership Seminar" (台美教育倡議：建立全球教育夥伴關係研討會) which was held at <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chung_Cheng_University" title="National Chung Cheng University" id="mwsA">National Chung Cheng University</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Wu" title="Joseph Wu" id="mwsQ">Joseph Wu</a> said that educational cooperation was the best way to establish cultural and social connections between Taiwan and the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States" title="United States" id="mwsg">US</a>. <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Oudkirk" title="Sandra Oudkirk" id="mwsw">Sandra Springer Oudkirk</a>, director of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Institute_in_Taiwan" title="American Institute in Taiwan" id="mwtA">American Institute in Taiwan</a>, said she hoped more <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans" title="Americans" id="mwtQ">Americans</a> would visit Taiwan to learn <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language" id="mwtg">Chinese</a>, teach English and help Taiwan achieve its goal of bilingual education by 2030.<sup about="#mwt53" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-13" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-13"}}'><a href="#cite_note-13" id="mwtw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwuA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwuQ">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwug">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="5" id="mwuw" aria-labelledby="Localization"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Localization">Localization</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Localization"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwvA">The bilingual policy may dilute the dominance of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Chinese" title="Standard Chinese" id="mwvQ">Standard Chinese</a> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandarin_Chinese" title="Mandarin Chinese" id="mwvg">Mandarin</a>) in Taiwan and amplify the cultural differences between <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Taiwan" title="Culture of Taiwan" id="mwvw">Taiwan</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China" title="Culture of the People's Republic of China" id="mwwA">Mainland China</a> without offending the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_China" title="Government of China" class="mw-redirect" id="mwwQ">Mainland Chinese government</a>.<sup about="#mwt54" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mwwg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwww"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwxA">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwxQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwyQ">In October 2017, the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_(Taiwan)" title="Ministry of Education (Taiwan)" id="mwyg">Ministry of Education</a> under the guidance of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_Ching-te" title="Lai Ching-te" id="mwyw">Lai Ching-te</a> established the "Committee on the Promotion of the English language" (英語推動會). In the following year, the committee drafted the "Report on the Promotion of English as an Official Language Policy" (我國推動英語成為官方語言政策報告).<sup about="#mwt57" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwzA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwzQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwzg">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwzw">]</span></span></a></sup> The original plan was to make <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mw0A">English</a> an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language" title="Official language" id="mw0Q">official language</a> of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan" title="Taiwan" id="mw0g">Taiwan</a>, but the cost of translating all government documents into English was too high. Because of this, the core of the policy shifted to making all Taiwanese bilingual in English and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language" id="mw0w">Chinese</a>.<sup about="#mwt58" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mw1A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw1Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw1g">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw1w">]</span></span></a></sup> 2019 was declared as "the first year of being a bilingual country" (雙語國家元年).<sup about="#mwt59" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-money_8-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"money"}}'><a href="#cite_note-money-8" id="mw2A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw2Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw2g">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw2w">]</span></span></a></sup> In 2021, the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Yuan" title="Executive Yuan" id="mw3A">Executive Yuan</a> proposed to establish an administrative body named the "Bilingual National Development Center" (雙語國家發展中心) to implement the policy, which has yet to be approved by the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Yuan" title="Legislative Yuan" id="mw3Q">Legislative Yuan</a>.<sup about="#mwt62" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-14" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-14"}}'><a href="#cite_note-14" id="mw3g"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw3w"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw4A">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw4Q">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<section data-mw-section-id="7" id="mw4g" aria-labelledby="Schools"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Schools">Schools</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Schools"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mw4w">In 2018, the Executive Yuan approved the bilingual policy, where in four years, it will invest more than <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Taiwan_dollar" title="New Taiwan dollar" id="mw5A">NT$</a>100 million worth of resources, of which more than 90% will be used for education.<sup about="#mwt65" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-15" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-15"}}'><a href="#cite_note-15" id="mw5Q"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw5g"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw5w">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw6A">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mw6Q">In 2021, a total of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Taiwan_dollar" title="New Taiwan dollar" id="mw6g">NT$</a>930 million will be invested in primary and secondary schools to set up bilingual experimental classes in 50 high schools; For universities, the budget is nearly <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Taiwan_dollar" title="New Taiwan dollar" id="mw6w">NT$</a>600 million. The appointed key training universities are <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University" title="National Taiwan University" id="mw7A">National Taiwan University</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_Normal_University" title="National Taiwan Normal University" id="mw7Q">National Taiwan Normal University</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Sun_Yat-sen_University" title="National Sun Yat-sen University" id="mw7g">National Sun Yat-sen University</a>, and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cheng_Kung_University" title="National Cheng Kung University" id="mw7w">National Cheng Kung University</a> while 41 key training departments and 37 universal enhancement universities were approved in 25 learning institutions.<sup about="#mwt68" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ct_16-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ct"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-ct-16"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ct-16" id="mw8A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw8Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw8g">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw8w">]</span></span></a></sup> The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_(Taiwan)" title="Ministry of Education (Taiwan)" id="mw9A">Ministry of Education</a> has also adopted the "Employment of Foreign English Teachers at Public Primary and Secondary Schools Assistance Plan" (協助公立國民中小學引進外籍英語教師計畫). Besides the original 81 foreign teachers, an additional 300 foreign teachers will be hired.<sup about="#mwt71" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-17" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-17"}}'><a href="#cite_note-17" id="mw9Q"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw9g"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw9w">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw-A">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mw-Q">The goal of the policy is that by 2024, 60% of primary and secondary schools will only use English in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medium_of_instruction" title="Medium of instruction" id="mw-g">teaching</a> English classes, and 1/7 of schools will be able to teach other subjects <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingualism" title="Bilingualism" class="mw-redirect" id="mw-w">bilingually</a>. By 2030, English classes in all high schools in the whole country will be taught solely in English, and one-third of schools will have <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingual_education" title="Bilingual education" id="mw_A">bilingual instruction</a> in other subjects. In 2024, 25% of sophomores in the funded universities previously mentioned need to reach <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_European_Framework_of_Reference_for_Languages" title="Common European Framework of Reference for Languages" id="mw_Q">CEFR</a> B2 in English, and at least 20% of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophomore" title="Sophomore" id="mw_g">sophomores</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freshman" title="Freshman" id="mw_w">freshmen</a> should have 20% of their credits allotted for English-only courses; By 2030, the figures 25%, 20%, and 20% need to reach 50%, 50%, and 50% respectively.<sup about="#mwt72" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ct_16-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ct"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ct-16" id="mwAQA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAQE"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQI">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQM">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwAQQ">In 2021, the primary and secondary school teachers in the front line believed that this policy lacked support. Even in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei" title="Taipei" id="mwAQU">Taipei</a>, where resources are relatively sufficient, there were still problems such as shortage of teachers and teaching materials.<sup about="#mwt75" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ltnpower_18-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ltnpower"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-ltnpower-18"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ltnpower-18" id="mwAQY"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAQc"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQg">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQk">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="8" id="mwAQo" aria-labelledby="Government_agencies"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Government_agencies">Government agencies</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Government agencies"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwAQs">In every agency, the deputy heads will form a "Bilingual Promotion Group" (雙語推動小組) that will promote bilingualism. The official websites of various ministries and commissions, documents and regulations related to foreigners, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procurement" title="Procurement" id="mwAQw">procurement</a> documents, front-line services, and public government information will be written bilingually. English translations of summaries of some <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indictment" title="Indictment" id="mwAQ0">indictments</a> and major judicial court judgments will be provided. Cultural and educational venues should promote bilingual services. <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_service" title="Civil service" id="mwAQ4">Civil servants</a> have to cultivate their English communication skills. The <a rel="mw:WikiLink/Interwiki" href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/全國技術士技能檢定" title="zh:全國技術士技能檢定" class="extiw" id="mwAQ8">National Technician Skills Test</a> must be in English, and the certificate will be issued bilingually.<sup about="#mwt78" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-19" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-19"}}'><a href="#cite_note-19" id="mwARA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwARE"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARI">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARM">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt79" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-lazypack_10-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"lazypack"}}'><a href="#cite_note-lazypack-10" id="mwARQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwARU"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARY">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARc">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="9" id="mwARg" aria-labelledby="Broadcasting"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Broadcasting">Broadcasting</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9" title="Edit section: Broadcasting"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwARk">An all-English-language <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_station" title="Television station" id="mwARo">television station</a> will be established and the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Broadcasting_System" title="Taiwan Broadcasting System" id="mwARs">Taiwan Broadcasting System</a> will be encouraged to broadcast English-language programs.<sup about="#mwt80" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-lazypack_10-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"lazypack"}}'><a href="#cite_note-lazypack-10" id="mwARw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAR0"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAR4">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAR8">]</span></span></a></sup> An integrated English learning and English <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation" title="Translation" id="mwASA">translation</a> resource platform will also be established.<sup about="#mwt81" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-lazypack_10-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"lazypack"}}'><a href="#cite_note-lazypack-10" id="mwASE"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwASI"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASM">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section></section><section data-mw-section-id="10" id="mwASU" aria-labelledby="Criticism"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Criticism">Criticism</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: Criticism"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<section data-mw-section-id="11" id="mwASY" aria-labelledby="Examples_in_other_countries"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Examples_in_other_countries">Examples in other countries</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=11" title="Edit section: Examples in other countries"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwASc">Some critics pointed out that even <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore" title="Singapore" id="mwASg">Singapore</a> had spent decades in "establishing a bilingual policy" (建立雙語政策), which compared to Taiwan seems unrealistic to "achieve bilingualism" (達成雙語) within just 12 years.<sup about="#mwt84" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mwASk"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwASo"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASs">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASw">]</span></span></a></sup> Singapore, which is also Taiwan's inspiration in establishing a successful bilingual society, also had a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_in_the_Straits_Settlements" title="Singapore in the Straits Settlements" id="mwAS0">British colonial history</a> and a practical need for <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Singapore" title="Demographics of Singapore" id="mwAS4">inter-ethnic communication</a> both of which that did not occur in Taiwan.<sup about="#mwt87" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-20" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-20"}}'><a href="#cite_note-20" id="mwAS8"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwATA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwATE">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwATI">]</span></span></a></sup> In addition, Taiwan's existing education system is extremely <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatism" title="Conservatism" id="mwATM">conservative</a> as it focuses on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rote_learning" title="Rote learning" id="mwATQ">rote memorization</a>. It also does not encourage the use of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mwATU">English</a> in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everyday_life" title="Everyday life" id="mwATY">daily life</a> and beliefs of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code-switching" title="Code-switching" id="mwATc">code-switching</a> between <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language" id="mwATg">Chinese</a> and English as <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boasting" title="Boasting" id="mwATk">showing off</a> or an implicit <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Status_symbol" title="Status symbol" id="mwATo">status symbol</a> are present in public discourse, such as the attitudes towards the code-switching trend, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/"Crystal_Style"?action=edit&amp;redlink=1' title='"Crystal Style" (page does not exist)' about="#mwt82" typeof="mw:Transclusion mw:LocalizedAttrs" class="new" id="mwATs" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"ill","href":"./Template:Ill"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"\"Crystal Style\""},"2":{"wt":"zh"},"3":{"wt":"晶晶體"}},"i":0}}]}' data-mw-i18n='{"title":{"lang":"x-page","key":"red-link-title","params":["\"Crystal Style\""]}}'>"Crystal Style"</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal; " about="#mwt82" id="mwATw"><span typeof="mw:Entity"> </span><span typeof="mw:Entity">[</span><a rel="mw:WikiLink/Interwiki" href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/晶晶體" title="zh:晶晶體" class="extiw">zh</a><span typeof="mw:Entity">]</span></span> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters" title="Traditional Chinese characters" about="#mwt83" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwAT0" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"zh","href":"./Template:Zh"},"params":{"t":{"wt":"晶晶體"},"p":{"wt":"jīngjīngtǐ"},"w":{"wt":"ching1-ching1-tʻi3"}},"i":0}}]}'>Chinese</a><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt83">:</span><span about="#mwt83"> </span><span lang="zh-Hant" about="#mwt83"><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_traditional_Chinese-language_text"/>晶晶體</span><span about="#mwt83">; </span><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin" title="Pinyin" about="#mwt83">pinyin</a><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt83">:</span><span about="#mwt83"> </span><i about="#mwt83"><span lang="zh-Latn">jīngjīngtǐ</span></i><span about="#mwt83">; </span><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade–Giles" title="Wade–Giles" about="#mwt83">Wade–Giles</a><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt83">:</span><span about="#mwt83"> </span><i about="#mwt83" id="mwAT4"><span lang="zh-Latn-wadegile">ching<sup>1</sup>-ching<sup>1</sup>-tʻi<sup>3</sup></span></i>). Therefore, critics in 2021 commented that this policy was unlikely to succeed under the current system.<sup about="#mwt88" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Turton_21-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Turton"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Turton-21" id="mwAT8"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAUA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAUE">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAUI">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt91" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-22" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-22"}}'><a href="#cite_note-22" id="mwAUM"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAUQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAUU">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAUY">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwAUc">Some students pointed out that for four consecutive years, 70% of students failed their English examinations, but the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Republic_of_China" title="Government of the Republic of China" id="mwAUg">Taiwanese government</a> still hopes to become a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingualism" title="Multilingualism" id="mwAUk">bilingual</a> country within eight years, which they believe is unlikely. Also, since the majority of parents in Taiwan only speak <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language" id="mwAUo">Chinese languages</a>, it is also difficult for children to learn English in a non-English speaking environment.</p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="12" id="mwAUs" aria-labelledby="Cost_efficiency"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Cost_efficiency">Cost efficiency</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=12" title="Edit section: Cost efficiency"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwAUw">This policy uses a huge amount of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_budget" title="Government budget" id="mwAU0">budget</a>. Opponents believe that these funds should have been used to improve <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_area" title="Rural area" id="mwAU4">rural</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education" title="Education" id="mwAU8">education</a>, provide more nutritious <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_meal" title="School meal" id="mwAVA">school meals</a>, or promote <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Taiwan" title="Languages of Taiwan" id="mwAVE">other languages</a> more commonly used in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan" title="Taiwan" id="mwAVI">Taiwan</a>. Some people against the policy take <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan" title="Japan" id="mwAVM">Japan</a> as an example, pointing out that although <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mwAVQ">English</a> is not popular, Japan still has great economic influence in the world, and countries with higher <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product" title="Gross domestic product" id="mwAVU">GDP</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_countries_by_GDP_per_capita" title="Lists of countries by GDP per capita" class="mw-redirect" id="mwAVY">per capita</a> than Taiwan, such as Japan, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea" title="South Korea" id="mwAVc">South Korea</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg" title="Luxembourg" id="mwAVg">Luxembourg</a>, and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iceland" title="Iceland" id="mwAVk">Iceland</a>, do not use English as an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language" title="Official language" id="mwAVo">official language</a>, doubting the real benefits of Taiwan's transformation into a bilingual country.<sup about="#mwt94" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-23" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-23"}}'><a href="#cite_note-23" id="mwAVs"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAVw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAV0">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAV4">]</span></span></a></sup> Foreign observers who have long lived in Taiwan think that for most <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_people" title="Taiwanese people" id="mwAV8">Taiwanese</a>, speaking English is not a necessary <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_skills" title="Life skills" id="mwAWA">life skill</a>, and those who need it can learn it on their own.<sup about="#mwt97" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Turton_21-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Turton"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-Turton-21"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Turton-21" id="mwAWE"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAWI"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAWM">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAWQ">]</span></span></a></sup> Taiwan Insight, a magazine of the Taiwan Studies Program at the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham" title="University of Nottingham" id="mwAWU">University of Nottingham</a>, also pointed out that strengthening English instruction may further widen the gap between resource-rich students from less-privileged students, and deemed the statement of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Mei-ling" title="Chen Mei-ling" id="mwAWY">Chen Mei-ling</a>, minister of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Development_Council_(Taiwan)" title="National Development Council (Taiwan)" id="mwAWc">National Development Council</a>, that online resources can solve the problem unreasonable.<sup about="#mwt98" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mwAWg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAWk"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAWo">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAWs">]</span></span></a></sup> Danyu Bai (柏單于), Deputy Director of the Trade and Investment Department of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Trade_Office_in_Taipei" title="Canadian Trade Office in Taipei" id="mwAWw">Canadian Trade Office in Taiwan</a>, said that fairness should be considered first in policy-making. From <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada" title="Canada" id="mwAW0">Canada</a>'s experience, bilingual education will increase the gap in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resource_allocation" title="Resource allocation" id="mwAW4">resource allocation</a> and have a negative effect on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_integration" title="Social integration" id="mwAW8">social integration</a>.<sup about="#mwt99" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ltnpower_18-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ltnpower"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ltnpower-18" id="mwAXA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAXE"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAXI">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAXM">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwAXQ">Chiang Wen-yu (江文瑜), a professor of linguistics at <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University" title="National Taiwan University" id="mwAXU">National Taiwan University</a>, and Her One-soon<!-- this is how his name is romanized on his personal website--> (何萬順), a professor of foreign languages at <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunghai_University" title="Tunghai University" id="mwAXY">Tunghai University</a>, believe that the English language skills of the Taiwanese people is already very good. Chiang asserts that Taiwanese people are already the most foreigner-friendly people in the world. Chiang suggests claims that Taiwanese people have poor English language skills is a fabrication, potentially emphasised by those who stand to gain from the demand for English lessons, such as language testing companies.<sup about="#mwt102" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-24" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-24"}}'><a href="#cite_note-24" id="mwAXc"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAXg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAXk">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAXo">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt103" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ltnJiangHe_5-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ltnJiangHe"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ltnJiangHe-5" id="mwAXs"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAXw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAX0">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAX4">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="13" id="mwAX8" aria-labelledby="Effect_on_other_languages"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Effect_on_other_languages">Effect on other languages</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=2030_Bilingual_Nation&amp;action=edit&amp;section=13" title="Edit section: Effect on other languages"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwAYA">The body of the policy proposed by the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ing-wen" title="Tsai Ing-wen" id="mwAYE">Tsai Ing-wen</a> government only talks about <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language" title="English language" id="mwAYI">English</a> and does not specify which other language is part of this "<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingualism" title="Multilingualism" id="mwAYM">bilingualism</a>" (雙語) project. It is generally believed that this language is <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Chinese" title="Standard Chinese" id="mwAYQ">Standard Chinese</a> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Mandarin" title="Taiwanese Mandarin" id="mwAYU">Mandarin</a>), the current mainstream <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca" title="Lingua franca" id="mwAYY">lingua franca</a> in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan" title="Taiwan" id="mwAYc">Taiwan</a>. However, Taiwan has many <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_language" title="Indigenous language" id="mwAYg">indigenous</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_language" title="Regional language" id="mwAYk">local languages</a>, and the <a rel="mw:WikiLink/Interwiki" href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/國家語言發展法" title="zh:國家語言發展法" class="extiw" id="mwAYo">National Languages Development Act</a> recognizes 20 <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_language" title="National language" id="mwAYs">national languages</a>, including aboriginal <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formosan_languages" title="Formosan languages" id="mwAYw">Formosan languages</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Hokkien" title="Taiwanese Hokkien" id="mwAY0">Taiwanese Hokkien</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Hakka" title="Taiwanese Hakka" id="mwAY4">Hakka</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Sign_Language" title="Taiwan Sign Language" id="mwAY8">Taiwan Sign Language</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Min" title="Eastern Min" id="mwAZA">Eastern Min</a> (i.e. <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matsu_dialect" title="Matsu dialect" id="mwAZE">Matsu dialect</a>), etc. In addition, migrant workers and new migrants also bring with them many languages from neighboring <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Asia" title="Southeast Asia" id="mwAZI">Southeast Asian countries</a>. There is still a debate on the impact of the bilingual policy on other languages. Some people believe that the bilingual policy will kill other languages, leading to the change of Taiwan's linguistic landscape from 20 languages to only recognizing two official languages, negating Taiwan's inherent <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiculturalism" title="Multiculturalism" id="mwAZM">multiculturalism</a>.<sup about="#mwt104" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwAZQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAZU"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAZY">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAZc">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt105" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ltnJiangHe_5-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ltnJiangHe"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ltnJiangHe-5" id="mwAZg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAZk"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAZo">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAZs">]</span></span></a></sup> However, some people believe that through the introduction of English, the dominance of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Chinese" title="Standard Chinese" id="mwAZw">Standard Chinese</a> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Mandarin" title="Taiwanese Mandarin" id="mwAZ0">Mandarin</a>) can be removed. The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Statebuilding_Party" title="Taiwan Statebuilding Party" id="mwAZ4">Taiwan Statebuilding Party</a> argues that the bilingual policy should eliminate the foreign <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Chinese" title="Standard Chinese" id="mwAZ8">Standard Chinese</a> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandarin_Chinese" title="Mandarin Chinese" id="mwAaA">Mandarin</a>) and choose another <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language" title="First language" id="mwAaE">native language</a> with English to account for bilingualism.<sup about="#mwt106" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwAaI"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAaM"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAaQ">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAaU">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwAaY">Some opponents worry that <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingual_education" title="Bilingual education" id="mwAac">bilingual education</a> will cause confusion among children, but there is no evidence that the bilingual environment itself will cause problems for children.<sup about="#mwt107" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-NewsLens_7-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"NewsLens"}}'><a href="#cite_note-NewsLens-7" id="mwAag"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAak"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAao">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAas">]</span></span></a></sup> Comprehensive studies have shown that bilingual education is beneficial and harmless to children.<sup about="#mwt110" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-25" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-25"}}'><a href="#cite_note-25" id="mwAaw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAa0"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAa4">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAa8">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwAbA">The Linguistic Society of Taiwan and the Taiwan Languages and Literature Society think that making English the second official language is a huge mistake and a form of self-colonization.<sup about="#mwt111" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-cw_1-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"cw"}}'><a href="#cite_note-cw-1" id="mwAbE"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAbI"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAbM">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAbQ">]</span></span></a></sup> Other opponents also hold to the idea that it adheres to <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Americans" title="Anglo-Americans" id="mwAbU">Anglo-American</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegemony" title="Hegemony" id="mwAbY">hegemony</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De-Sinicization" title="De-Sinicization" id="mwAbc">de-sinicization</a>, or a weak version of an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_independence_movement" title="Taiwan independence movement" id="mwAbg">independent Taiwan</a>. The editors of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Times" title="Liberty Times" id="mwAbk">Liberty Times</a> (which has <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_Times" title="Taipei Times" id="mwAbo">an English version</a>, similar to Japanese newspapers) deem that such criticisms are not worth one's attention.<sup about="#mwt112" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-LtnEditorial_11-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"LtnEditorial"}}'><a href="#cite_note-LtnEditorial-11" id="mwAbs"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAbw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAb0">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAb4">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
