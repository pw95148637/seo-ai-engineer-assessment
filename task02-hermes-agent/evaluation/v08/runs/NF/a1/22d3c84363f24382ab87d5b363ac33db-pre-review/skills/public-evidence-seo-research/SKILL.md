# Public-evidence competitor SEO research

## Workflow
1. Start with `research_status` when using the research suite. Apply scoped, unexpired lessons; read successful current-run controller verifications with `research_evidence` instead of refetching. Note budgets and stop conditions.
2. State falsifiable hypotheses before tool selection: which pages target the terms, how internal links connect them, whether independent sites really mention/link to the competitor, and what gaps our own site could test. Never assume rankings or strategy effectiveness.
3. Batch independent homepage fetch and initial searches. Give every fetch a purpose naming the hypothesis and possible counterevidence. Follow discovered links to the hub, core generic/brand pages, and a contrasting sample; do not accumulate URLs as a proxy for quality.
4. Inspect title, H1, description, canonical, robots, visible sections, authorship, dates, sources, FAQ, internal anchors, and commercial destinations. Separate metadata configuration from actual indexation, rankings, rich-result eligibility, and performance.
5. Test content-quality judgments against another brand or discovered long-tail page. Compare substantive answers, not section counts. Record conflicting figures with conditions; figures without comparable samples are unexplained discrepancies, not proof of fabrication. Shared structure supports a template inference, not automatic AI-generation, penalty, or cannibalization claims.
6. Search domain and brand variants for external evidence within the search limit. Check collisions with tickers, product codes, or unrelated entities. Snippets identify candidates, not verified endorsements. After empty results change strategy rather than repeatedly tightening the same query; no results does not prove no backlinks.
7. Read a few relevant search-listed external pages with `research_offpage`; one ambiguous candidate can be checked to establish a false positive. Record actual target URL, anchor, rel, context, and relevance. Treat `outbound_hosts` as outgoing links only. A shared tracked destination can support a funnel inference, not ownership, commission, or incoming-link claims. Separate off-page observations, inferences, and unknowns.
8. Revise hypotheses explicitly when counterevidence arrives. Respect robots and access denials without bypass or repeated retries. Stop at budget or after two steps yield no new evidence; report narrow coverage rather than inventing performance or backlink data.
9. Rank actions for OUR site, not the competitor. Start with our inventory, legal/compliance scope, and authorized baseline data; label missing dependencies. Prefer a small evidence-rich core-page test before mass long-tail publishing. Separate implementation cost, evidence quality, and unknown return. Define measurements using our authorized indexation, query-to-page mapping, impressions/clicks, and relevant citations—not invented uplift.
10. Before `research_finish`, check every observation/inference against actual current-run content. Failed retrieval/search evidence supports access claims only. Include on-page and off-page claims with kind, topic, and evidence IDs; use unknowns when coverage is insufficient. Return conclusions, counterevidence, limitations, prioritized actions, and direct source links in the user's language.

## Standing evidence gates
- Keep competitor public evidence separate from our private analytics and proposed changes; never imply access to competitor Search Console or analytics.
- Distinguish page-reported article totals and claimed firsthand testing from independently verified quantity and quality.
- Treat editorial-team or organization bylines only as team attribution, not as named authors, identifiable author information, demonstrated expertise, or professional endorsement. Claim a named author only when the evidence identifies an individual; assess credentials separately. Keep compound claims granular so practical details or update labels do not lend unsupported credibility to authorship claims.
- Bound negative findings to inspected pages and extraction: absent from sampled visible content does not mean absent across the site.
- Do not infer no backlinks, low authority, domain transfer, or successful link building from sparse search results.
- For gambling reviews, distinguish transport encryption from operator legitimacy or payout safety. Do not endorse unverified safety or manufacture transaction tests. Favor sourced claims, commercial disclosure, jurisdiction review, and risk information without inducing deposits or sensitive-document submission.
- Keep progress updates brief and decision-focused; final tables connect evidence to interpretation and actions rather than replaying tool calls.
