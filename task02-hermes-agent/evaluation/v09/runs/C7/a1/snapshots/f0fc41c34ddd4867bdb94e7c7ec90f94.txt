    <p class="sub-zh">臺灣的國家級政策:厚植全民英語力、提升國家競爭力,目標 2030 年。</p>

<!-- INTRO -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="wrap prose">
    <p class="lead">Building on the advantage Taiwan already holds in Mandarin, the <strong>Bilingual 2030</strong> policy aims to further strengthen citizens' English communication ability and Taiwan's global competitiveness. It is led by the <strong>National Development Council</strong> (overall coordination) and the <strong>Ministry of Education</strong> (delivery in schools).</p>
    <p class="lead-zh">立基於臺灣既有的華語優勢,「<strong>2030 雙語政策</strong>」旨在進一步強化國人的英文溝通能力與全球競爭力。由<strong>國家發展委員會</strong>統籌、<strong>教育部</strong>負責教育推行。</p>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- TWO VISIONS -->
<section class="section" style="padding-top:0;">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section-head"><h2>Two Visions</h2><p>兩大願景</p></div>
    <div class="vision-grid">
      <div class="vision-card">
        <div class="n">Vision 1</div>
        <h3>厚植國人英語力</h3>
        <div class="en">Strengthen the English ability of all citizens</div>
        <p>Raise everyone's real, communicative English — not English as an exam subject, but English the workforce can use to connect with the world.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="vision-card">
        <div class="n">Vision 2</div>
        <h3>提升國家競爭力</h3>
        <div class="en">Raise the nation's global competitiveness</div>
        <p>Help Taiwan's industries connect to global markets, attract international enterprise, and create high-quality jobs and economic growth.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- DUAL TRACK -->
<section class="section" style="padding-top:0;">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section-head"><h2>A dual-track approach</h2><p>雙軌並進</p></div>
    <div class="track">
      <div class="track-card">
        <h3>全面提升</h3>
        <div class="en">General improvement</div>
        <p>Comprehensively raise the everyday English communication ability of all young people — every student, every school.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="track-card">
        <h3>重點培育</h3>
        <div class="en">Focused cultivation</div>
        <p>Nurture an elite group of students in key professional fields who can operate fully in English on the international stage.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- SIX STRATEGIES -->
<section class="section" style="padding-top:0;">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section-head"><h2>Six education strategies</h2><p>教育推動六大策略</p></div>
    <div class="focus-grid">
      <div class="focus-card">
        <span class="ic">🍎</span>
        <h3>加速教學活化及生活化</h3>
        <div class="en">Make teaching livelier and tied to real life</div>
        <p>Move English from a textbook subject toward using English to learn and do real things.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="focus-card">
        <span class="ic">👩‍🏫</span>
        <h3>擴增英語人力資源</h3>
        <div class="en">Expand English teaching manpower</div>
        <p>Grow the pool of English-capable teachers and foreign-teacher support across schools.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="focus-card">
        <span class="ic">📱</span>
        <h3>善用科技普及個別化學習</h3>
        <div class="en">Use technology for personalized learning</div>
        <p>Use digital tools to widen access and narrow the urban–rural resource gap.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="focus-card">
        <span class="ic">🌏</span>
        <h3>促進教育體系國際化</h3>
        <div class="en">Internationalize the education system</div>
        <p>Build international exchange, sister schools and a more globally connected campus.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="focus-card">
        <span class="ic">⚖️</span>
        <h3>鬆綁法規建立彈性機制</h3>
        <div class="en">Loosen regulations, build flexibility</div>
        <p>Adjust rules so schools can innovate and adopt bilingual teaching more freely.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="focus-card">
        <span class="ic">💡</span>
        <h3>創新學習模式</h3>
        <div class="en">Innovate learning models</div>
        <p>Encourage new bilingual and content-and-language approaches in the classroom.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

      <h3>Where this hub comes in · 這個資源網的角色</h3>
      <p>The policy is ambitious — and bilingual teaching is still new to many teachers. The gap is rarely willingness; it is ready-to-use materials and confidence.</p>
      <p>政策目標宏大,但雙語教學對許多老師仍是新事物。缺的往往不是意願,而是「能直接上手的素材」與信心。</p>
      <p>The Taiwan Bilingual Hub exists to close that gap — bilingual campus pages, classroom resources and international partners that teachers at any level can use tomorrow.</p>
      <p>臺灣雙語資源網,就是為了補上這道落差——提供各級學校可立即使用的雙語校園網頁、課堂素材與國際夥伴。</p>
